The show

Desh Premi & Son is an animated Hindi series. Every week a father, Desh Premi, and his son, Videsh Premi, argue about a news story that matters at home: money, health, jobs, prices, scams. The facts are the referee: every claim is sourced, and the sources are listed under each episode.

AI Voices & Art • Facts Sourced The characters and their voices are AI-generated and fictional. Real public figures appear only through their quoted, dated words.

Desh Premi Publisher

Desh Premi Publisher is the show's own publishing tool. It is used only by the show's creator, on the show's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it. It:

  • uploads our own episodes and Shorts to our own YouTube channel and schedules them, and adds their thumbnails, Hindi captions and playlists;
  • reads our own channel's analytics and reports, to learn which episodes help viewers understand the news;
  • posts our own videos to our own Instagram, Facebook Page and X accounts, only after the creator approves each post;
  • reads the public comments on our own posts, to learn what confused viewers or what they want next.

It never reads, posts to or changes anyone else's account. How it handles data is set out in the privacy policy.
