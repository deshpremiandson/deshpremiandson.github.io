Privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026

Who we are

Desh Premi & Son is an animated Hindi news-commentary series made by one independent creator in India. This policy covers this website and Desh Premi Publisher, the show's own publishing tool (shown on Google's consent screen as "deshpremi-publisher"). Contact: deshpremison@gmail.com.

Who uses Desh Premi Publisher

Only the show's creator, on the show's own accounts: the YouTube channel @deshpremiandson, the Instagram account @deshpremiandson, the Facebook Page "Desh Premi & Son" and the X account @deshpremiandson. The tool is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

Google and YouTube data

Desh Premi Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using our YouTube content you are also covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

With the creator's permission, the tool accesses only the show's own channel:

  • Publishing: it uploads and schedules our own videos and Shorts, and sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, Hindi captions and playlists, and our own pinned or first comments (YouTube scope youtube.force-ssl);
  • Reading: our own channel's details, videos and public comments on our own videos (youtube.readonly);
  • Analytics: our own channel's YouTube Analytics and Reporting data, such as views, watch time, traffic sources and shares (yt-analytics.readonly).

How we use it: only to publish the show's videos and to measure how they perform, so we can make clearer episodes. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone, except the cloud provider that runs the tool for us (below).

Desh Premi Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Instagram, Facebook and X data

In the same way, the tool uses the official Meta (Instagram and Facebook) and X APIs only on the show's own accounts: to post our own videos after the creator approves each post, and to read the statistics and public comments of our own posts. It does not read, post to or change anyone else's account, and it sends no messages to other people.

Viewers and commenters

  • We see only the totals the platforms report, such as views, watch time, likes and shares. We do not receive the identity of individual viewers.
  • We read public comments on our own posts to understand feedback. Comment text received from YouTube is deleted or refreshed within 30 days, as YouTube's developer policies require. We keep only our own notes, such as "three viewers asked about fixed deposits".
  • This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Storage and security

  • The access tokens the creator grants are stored encrypted in Amazon Web Services (AWS Systems Manager Parameter Store, Sydney region) and on the creator's own computer. They are never published or shared.
  • Post records and performance numbers are kept in the tool's private database in the same AWS account.
  • Only the creator can approve or publish posts.

Keeping, deleting and revoking

  • Tokens are deleted when access is revoked or the tool is retired. Performance numbers are kept for as long as the show runs.
  • Access to a Google account can be revoked at any time on the Google account permissions page (Google security settings).
  • If you believe we hold something about you, for example a comment of yours, email us and we will delete it.

Children

The show is made for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
